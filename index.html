Toronto 2021 Census: Where the City Lives

An analysis of how Toronto's population of about 2.79 million is spread across the city's 158 neighbourhoods and 4 administrative divisions, using 2021 Census data from Statistics Canada and the City of Toronto.

  • Question: Which neighbourhoods and administrative divisions hold the most people, and how even is the spread across the city?
  • Data prep (Python, pandas): loaded the census profile data, cleaned and reshaped it, and matched neighbourhoods to their administrative divisions.
  • Dashboard (Power BI): built an interactive report comparing population by neighbourhood and administrative division, with rankings and a map.
